Red-Lamp Console — Information-Limited CT Denoising

Synthetic demonstration on a signal-known-exactly detection task (120 kV, 3 mm lesion). The lamp is the input gauge: the analytic ideal-observer detectability of the unprocessed input against a prespecified requirement (Rose, d′ = 5) — the operational floor. It is a task requirement, not a zero-information boundary: information remains below it, but no post-processing can bring the requirement back into reach. A method and its demonstration, not a scanner calibration — the coefficients are a documented relative model.
